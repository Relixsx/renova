# Daily and arrival message editing

Use [FULL_REMINDER_MESSAGE_EDITOR.md](./FULL_REMINDER_MESSAGE_EDITOR.md) for the current installer and complete instructions.

The customer view contains a large, editable full paragraph with the wording, items, quantities and amount. It replaces the earlier short paragraph editor and read-only customer preview. Clear, type or paste a replacement and save the selected message. A blank or unfinished draft remains visible for editing; validation no longer restores the old text while typing.

Daily and arrival wording are independent. The first confirmation has a separate automatic read-only preview. Selecting Email exposes its subject and heading fields; email and SMS use the saved full paragraph without appending the old order amount. Editing a reminder does not change the stored order total or products.

Expected Jumia dates and the bold countdown are automatic outside the editable paragraph. The countdown uses the current Nigeria date on every send: 9 days, then 8, then 7 before a 6 November window start. The date window stays visible. Arrival requires verified courier information and explicit confirmation; an estimated date cannot start it.

The approved Meta greeting and closing remain fixed. Printed STOP instructions appear in arrival reminders only; genuine customer opt-outs remain effective at every stage. Saving, previewing or filtering does not start reminders, send a message or change the 24-hour schedule. Running/Stopped filters and owner stop controls remain available.

No new migration is required after the installed 0007 migration. The current bundle includes the earlier layout and reminder filters.
